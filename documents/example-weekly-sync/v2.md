# Moving the weekly sync

**To:** the product team

From next week, the weekly sync moves from Monday at 9:00 to Tuesday at 10:00 and gets shorter. The new slot leaves Monday morning free for planning.

If the new time does not work for you, reply in the team channel by Friday so we can look for another option.
